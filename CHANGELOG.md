# Changelog

All notable changes to Jaunt. Versions match the plugin version shown in `/xlplugins`.

## Unreleased

- Listing browser (`/jaunt`): venues and events grouped by day, "Open now" first, then today, tomorrow and the coming dates, in your local time, with the selected listing's details beside the list. Venues show their current and next opening; one-off events show up to 30 days ahead; Party Finder ads without a time count as open while they are listed. Copy an address, travel there with Lifestream if it is installed, or open the listing's page. The browser remembers the listing you selected. Party Finder venue ads are read while you browse the Party Finder's None category. The cog in the title bar opens Settings.
- Venues from [FFXIV Venues](https://ffxivvenues.com) that open in the coming week in the browser, fetched when you open it at most every 8 hours, with their address, website, Discord and whether they are open 24/7. A favourited, visited or hidden venue with no opening that week stays listed.
- Party Finder venue ads from [xivpf](https://xivpf.com) in the browser, fetched when you open it at most every 10 minutes (the refresh cooldown), so ads show without opening the Party Finder. An ad you have also seen in the game shows once.
- Events from [Partake](https://partake.gg) in the browser until they end, fetched when you open it at most every 8 hours, with their times, world and data centre, location and the host's website and Discord. Jaunt is not affiliated with or endorsed by Partake.
- Roleplay events from the [FFXIV RP Event Calendar](https://ffxiv-rp.org/) in the browser, fetched when you open it at most every 8 hours, with their address or open-world location, page link and next date. Credited with a link to the calendar's CC BY-SA 4.0 licence.
- The browser fetches and keeps only listings on worlds your character can travel to (its home world's region), and fetches nothing until you log in. FFXIV Venues asks for each of your region's data centres, so logging in a character in another region fetches its venues at once; xivpf, Partake and the FFXIV RP Event Calendar can't be asked for one region, so they fetch that region's listings once their cooldown passes. The list shows only your region's listings, and switches when you change character.
- The browser shows a venue once when it is listed more than once at the same time: a re-posted Party Finder ad, a venue's several ads, or an ad for a venue on FFXIV Venues, Partake or the RP calendar at the same address. The merged entry takes the FFXIV Venues name and lists every link. An ad giving only the ward joins the venue in that ward open at the same time. An ad that gives a time with no time zone uses the zone of the venue it advertises.
- Settings window (`/jaunt config`): a refresh cooldown for fetched listing sources (default 10 minutes; some sources wait longer), 12- or 24-hour times and a date format, Clear cached listings, a list of hidden venues with Un-hide buttons, and the sources listings come from, each linked to its site, with their credits. Settings save as you change them. Listings that aren't favourited, visited or hidden are dropped from the cache a month after they were last seen.

- Groundwork for reading venue addresses from Party Finder ads: Jaunt now knows worlds, districts and the shorthand players use for them. Nothing visible changes yet.
- Groundwork for opening a venue's website: Jaunt can now find the links in Party Finder ads, including ones written without `https://` such as `discord.gg/…` or `venue.carrd.co`. Nothing visible changes yet.
- Groundwork for copying addresses and travelling to venues: Jaunt can now write an address as text to copy and turn it into a Lifestream destination, and keeps each Party Finder ad's text in its listing cache. Nothing visible changes yet.
- Groundwork for venues from FFXIV Venues: Jaunt can now read a venue's name and housing address from its venue list, correcting misspelt worlds. Nothing visible changes yet.
- Groundwork for venue links and opening hours: Jaunt can now read a venue's website and Discord from FFXIV Venues, and tell when a venue is open 24/7. Nothing visible changes yet.
- Groundwork for Party Finder ads from xivpf: Jaunt can now read the venue ads in xivpf's listings and tell when the game has sent the same ad. Nothing visible changes yet.
- Groundwork for events from Partake: Jaunt can now read upcoming FFXIV events from Partake's API, with their times, world, location and host links, and keep them in its listing cache. Nothing visible changes yet.
- Groundwork for events from the FFXIV RP Event Calendar: Jaunt can now read the calendar's roleplay events, with their dates, location, page link, rating and when they were last validated, keep them in its listing cache and work out each event's title and address from them. Nothing visible changes yet.
- Groundwork for venue names with decorative characters: Jaunt can now rewrite text its font can't draw, such as small capitals or styled letters, into characters it can. Nothing visible changes yet.
- Groundwork for filtering listings: Jaunt can now tag each venue, event and Party Finder ad with what it offers (clubs and DJs, bars, cafes, live music, games and gambling, roleplay, spas, art, photography, tarot, chill) and tell whether it is SFW or NSFW, from keywords in its text, FFXIV Venues' and Partake's own tags, and each source's SFW flag or age rating. Nothing visible changes yet.
