# Changelog

All notable changes to Jaunt. Versions match the plugin version shown in `/xlplugins`.

## Unreleased

- Listing browser (`/jaunt`): venues and events grouped by day, "Open now" first, then today, tomorrow and the coming dates, in your local time, with the selected listing's details beside the list. "Open now" lists the listings closing soonest first, counting one with no closing time as closing 3 hours after it opened, and 24/7 venues last. Venues show their current and next opening; one-off events show up to 30 days ahead; Party Finder ads without a time count as open while they are listed. Copy an address, travel there with Lifestream if it is installed, or open the listing's page. The browser remembers the listing you selected. Party Finder venue ads are read while you browse the Party Finder's None category. The cog in the title bar opens Settings.
- Venues from [FFXIV Venues](https://ffxivvenues.com) that open in the coming week in the browser, fetched when you open it at most every 8 hours, with their address, website, Discord and whether they are open 24/7. A favourited, visited or hidden venue with no opening that week stays listed.
- Party Finder venue ads from [xivpf](https://xivpf.com) in the browser, fetched when you open it at most every 10 minutes (the refresh cooldown), so ads show without opening the Party Finder. An ad you have also seen in the game shows once.
- Events from [Partake](https://partake.gg) in the browser until they end, fetched when you open it at most every 8 hours, with their times, world, location and the host's website and Discord. Jaunt is not affiliated with or endorsed by Partake.
- Roleplay events from the [FFXIV RP Event Calendar](https://ffxiv-rp.org/) in the browser, fetched when you open it at most every 8 hours, with their address or open-world location, page link and next date. Credited with a link to the calendar's CC BY-SA 4.0 licence.
- The browser fetches and keeps only listings on worlds your character can travel to (its home world's region), and fetches nothing until you log in. FFXIV Venues asks for each of your region's data centres, so logging in a character in another region fetches its venues at once; xivpf, Partake and the FFXIV RP Event Calendar can't be asked for one region, so they fetch that region's listings once their cooldown passes. The list shows only your region's listings, and switches when you change character.
- The browser shows a venue once when it is listed more than once at the same time: a re-posted Party Finder ad, a venue's several ads, or an ad for a venue on FFXIV Venues, Partake or the RP calendar at the same address. The merged entry takes the FFXIV Venues name and lists every link. An ad giving only the ward joins the venue in that ward open at the same time. An ad that gives a time with no time zone uses the zone of the venue it advertises.
- A listing's About shows the description from FFXIV Venues, Partake or the RP calendar when no Party Finder ad gives text, as plain text with its line breaks. A Discord invite in it becomes the listing's Discord link. Long descriptions are cut at 1,500 characters, with a link to read the rest on the listing's website. Discord handles in RP calendar descriptions are removed and never stored.
- Listing titles stand out in a larger font in the browser's list and detail. The detail footer links a venue's Discord and each source's site ("Discord · Sources: FFXIV Venues, xivpf"), and the browser shows how many listings it lists.
- Listings show an icon in the browser's list and a banner image above their details: the venue's Discord server icon, the icon of the website it gives on FFXIV Venues or Partake, or its FFXIV Venues banner, and the FFXIV Venues banner, the Discord server's banner or the Partake event's image. Images load as listings scroll into view and are kept on disk, so reopening the browser shows them without downloading again. Websites in Party Finder ads are never fetched, so an ad's author can't see who scrolled past it. A listing without an icon shows a pictogram of its main tag (a mug for a cafe, a martini glass for a bar), and one without a banner shows the game's loading screen for its housing district or the zone it is in, else the world map.
- Favourite, mark visited or hide a listing from the buttons beside its title in the browser. Marks stay with the venue's address, so every later listing there carries them, from any source, including new Party Finder ads; each button's tooltip names the address. A mark follows a venue that moves on FFXIV Venues, or whose website turns up at a new address once the old one stops showing it. Visited on a Partake or RP calendar event marks just that event. Favourites and visited show as a star and a tick in the list. Hiding a venue also hides its ads, and Settings lists it once with its address.
- Search and filter the browser's list: words in the search box must all match a listing's name, text or tags, `-word` leaves out listings with it and `"two words"` matches a phrase. Chips narrow it to SFW listings (on by default), favourites, or listings with every tag you pick, leave out visited ones, or bring back hidden venues. Filters and search are remembered when you reopen the browser, and the list says when nothing matches.
- A listing's details show a Lineup of the DJs, bards and acts its Party Finder ads name ("DJs: KK, Moon Cat, and Sleepy", "Music by …"), each linking to their Twitch channel when an ad links it. Names an ad doesn't mark as performers, such as a birthday or a list of hosts, are left out.
- A listing's details show its age or rating and whether it counts as SFW or NSFW ("18+ · SFW", "Mature · SFW"), and its tags.
- Settings window (`/jaunt config`): a refresh cooldown for fetched listing sources (default 10 minutes; some sources wait longer), 12- or 24-hour times and a date format, Clear cached listings, a list of hidden venues with Un-hide buttons, and the sources listings come from, each linked to its site, with their credits. Settings save as you change them. Listings that aren't favourited, visited or hidden are dropped from the cache a month after they were last seen.

- Groundwork for reading venue addresses from Party Finder ads: Jaunt now knows worlds, districts and the shorthand players use for them. Nothing visible changes yet.
- Groundwork for opening a venue's website: Jaunt can now find the links in Party Finder ads, including ones written without `https://` such as `discord.gg/…` or `venue.carrd.co`. Nothing visible changes yet.
- Groundwork for copying addresses and travelling to venues: Jaunt can now write an address as text to copy and turn it into a Lifestream destination, and keeps each Party Finder ad's text in its listing cache. Nothing visible changes yet.
- Groundwork for venues from FFXIV Venues: Jaunt can now read a venue's name and housing address from its venue list, correcting misspelt worlds. Nothing visible changes yet.
- Groundwork for venue links and opening hours: Jaunt can now read a venue's website and Discord from FFXIV Venues, and tell when a venue is open 24/7. Nothing visible changes yet.
- Groundwork for Party Finder ads from xivpf: Jaunt can now read the venue ads in xivpf's listings and tell when the game has sent the same ad. Nothing visible changes yet.
- Groundwork for events from Partake: Jaunt can now read upcoming FFXIV events from Partake's API, with their times, world, location and host links, and keep them in its listing cache. Nothing visible changes yet.
- Groundwork for events from the FFXIV RP Event Calendar: Jaunt can now read the calendar's roleplay events, with their dates, location, page link, rating and when they were last validated, keep them in its listing cache and work out each event's title and address from them. Nothing visible changes yet.
- Groundwork for venue names with decorative characters: Jaunt can now rewrite text its font can't draw, such as small capitals or styled letters, into characters it can. Nothing visible changes yet.
- Groundwork for filtering listings: Jaunt can now tag each venue, event and Party Finder ad with what it offers (clubs and DJs, bars, cafes, live music, games, gambling, roleplay, spas, art, photography, tarot, chill) and tell whether it is SFW or NSFW, from keywords in its text, FFXIV Venues' and Partake's own tags, and each source's SFW flag or age rating. Nothing visible changes yet.
